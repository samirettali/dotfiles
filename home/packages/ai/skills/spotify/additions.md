## Personal recommendations and playlist filing

Read [Samir's listening profile](listening-profile.md) when choosing music,
extending the queue, finding unfamiliar tracks, or filing tracks into playlists.
It contains taste, playlist taxonomy, and the distinct Extend/Discovery workflows.
Resolve the path relative to this skill.

For playback status, queue inspection, or an explicit track/playlist operation
that needs no taste or filing decision, skip the profile. Do not fetch listening
history or top items just to perform those operations.
